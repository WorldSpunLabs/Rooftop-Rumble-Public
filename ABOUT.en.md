# Rooftop Rumble — Demo 01: ArmPits

A bear drummer, a horse in a coat, and a man with a score to settle with reality. Three opponents stand in their way, each more stubborn than the last. It’s going to be a loud evening.

A small single-player fighter by **world spun labs** with one universal block, generous reaction windows, punches, kicks, heavy attacks, grabs, and a three-section energy meter. Land hits, build a combo, and spend your energy on a signature weapon or a super.

- Six animated fighters and two routes of three fights each.
- Ten illustrated arenas, including a rooftop at night, IT Fest, a server room in crisis, a secret avocado plantation, and a yacht deck.
- First-to-two matches, increasingly tough AI, and free play with all 36 fighter pairings, including blue-tinted mirror matches.
- 60-second rounds, guard stamina, a brief hold before each throw, and knockback from heavy hits.
- Remappable keys, separate music and effects volume, and progress saved in your browser.

The game uses AI-generated art, models, music, sound effects, and announcer voice. Base animations by Quaternius (CC0). Built with Babylon.js. Manrope and Russo One are licensed under the SIL Open Font License. Credits and licenses are included in the build.
